## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Tap a tube to select it, then tap another tube to pour. Liquids can only be poured into a tube that is empty or has the same color on top. The goal is to sort all liquids so each tube contains only one color.

**How many levels are there?**
SortVision features 1,000 puzzles across 10 difficulty tiers: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster, and Legend.

### Stars & Scoring

**How do I earn 3 stars?**
Complete a level in the optimal (fewest) number of moves. Star thresholds are based on the verified optimal solution for each puzzle.

**How are stars calculated?**
- **3 stars:** Complete in the optimal number of moves
- **2 stars:** Complete in up to 1.5 times the optimal moves
- **1 star:** Complete the level in any number of moves

### Controls

**iPhone & iPad**
- Tap a tube to select it.
- Tap another tube to pour.
- Tap an empty area to deselect.

**Mac**
- Click a tube to select it.
- Click another tube to pour.
- Click an empty area to deselect.

**Apple Vision Pro**
- Look at a tube to highlight it.
- Tap to select and pour.

### Troubleshooting

**The app is crashing or not responding.**
1. Force-quit the app and reopen it.
2. Ensure your device is running the latest OS version.
3. Update SortVision to the latest version from the App Store.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).

**No sound.**
- Check that your device is not in Silent Mode.
- Verify that Sound Effects are enabled in the in-game Settings.

**A level seems unsolvable.**
Every level has a verified solution. Try undoing your moves and taking a different approach.
