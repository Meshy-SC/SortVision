# Privacy Policy for SortVision

**Last Updated:** March 20, 2026

## Overview

SortVision ("the App") is a water sorting puzzle game developed by Meshy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

SortVision does **not** collect, store, or transmit any user data to our servers. We have no servers, no databases, and no access to your information.

### We Do NOT Collect
- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Usage analytics or tracking
- Advertising identifiers
- Any data whatsoever

## Data Storage

All your data remains entirely under your control:
1. **Locally** on your device

Game progress — including level completions, star ratings, move counts, and settings — is stored using Apple's on-device storage (UserDefaults). Your data never leaves your device.

We have **zero access** to your data.

## Third-Party Services

SortVision does **not** integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising frameworks, or tracking services. The App uses only Apple first-party frameworks.

## Data Sharing

We do **not**:
- Sell your data
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for advertising
- Track your behavior

## Required Reason APIs

In accordance with Apple's privacy requirements, the App declares usage of the following APIs in its privacy manifest:

| API Category | Reason | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| UserDefaults | CA92.1 | Saving game settings and preferences |

## Children's Privacy

SortVision does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It is suitable for all ages and complies with COPPA and similar regulations worldwide.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information to access, correct, or delete. You can:
- **Delete your data** by removing the app (local data is erased)

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
