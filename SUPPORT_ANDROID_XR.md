# SortVision Support — Android XR

SortVision is a colour-sorting (water sort) puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

SortVision opens as a window in your space. Point at a tube with your hand (or look at it, or use a controller) and pinch to select it.

- **The goal:** sort the liquids so every colour ends up in a tube of its own. A level is solved when each tube holds one colour and no colour is split across two tubes.
- **Pour:** pinch a tube to pick it up, then pinch another tube to pour its top colour into it. Pinch the raised tube again, or the empty space, to put it down.
- **Like onto like:** liquid only goes into an empty tube or onto the same colour, and only while there is room. Each tube holds four layers; as much as fits pours across.
- **Mystery layers:** from the Gold world on, some layers are hidden behind a "?" until the liquid above them has been poured away.
- **Undo** takes back your last pour and its move. There is no timer and there are no lives.
- **Stars and score:** every pour is a move. Solving within the level's par earns three stars (10 points), within one and a half times par two stars (7 points), otherwise one star (5 points). Your best result on each level counts.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb plays the level's solution, pour by pour. The first time on a level it uses one solution credit; replays of that level are free. Watching it earns no stars: afterwards the level starts over for you to solve. Pinch the lightbulb again (it shows a square while playing) to stop.
- **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, from five tubes and three colours up to fourteen tubes and twelve colours.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, each level opens when you solve the one before it.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, SortVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Solution credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in SortVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install SortVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels, stars and best moves. Purchases and credits stay.
