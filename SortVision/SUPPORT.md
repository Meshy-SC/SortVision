## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I play?
Tap a tube to select it, then tap another tube to pour. Match all colors to complete the level.

### How do I unlock new worlds?
Complete all levels in the current world to unlock the next one. Worlds unlock sequentially.

### Can I undo a move?
Yes! Tap the undo button (↶) in the bottom toolbar during gameplay.

### How are stars calculated?
- ⭐⭐⭐ Complete at or below the optimal move count
- ⭐⭐ Complete within 1.5× the optimal move count
- ⭐ Complete the level

### How do I reset my progress?
Go to Settings → Support & Data → Reset All Progress. **Warning:** This action cannot be undone.
