# SortVision Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 20, 2026

## Overview

SortVision ("the App") is developed by Ahmed DALHI. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

## Data Collection

**SortVision does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The App does not use:

- Analytics or tracking SDKs
- Advertising frameworks
- Third-party data collection services
- User accounts or authentication
- Network requests to external servers

## Data Stored on Device

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only** using standard system storage (UserDefaults):

- Game progress (completed levels, star ratings, best move counts)
- Sound preference (on/off)

This data never leaves your device and is not shared with any third party.

## Required Reason API Usage

The App uses the following Apple Required Reason APIs:

- **UserDefaults** (Reason CA92.1): Used to read and write app preferences and game progress data that is only accessible to the App itself.

## Third-Party Services

SortVision does not integrate any third-party SDKs, frameworks, or services.

## Children's Privacy

SortVision does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
